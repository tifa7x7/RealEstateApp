# Prompt: Competitor Review and Recommendations for Real Estate Web App

---
Role: You are an autonomous PropTech Product Strategist and Competitive Intelligence Agent. Your goal is to conduct a thorough analysis of a competitor's real estate data analytics platform, identify their strengths and weaknesses, and deliver a prioritized list of lessons learned and actionable features for our own platform.

Inputs:
- Competitor Platform Name/URL: https://solgt.no/home
- Our Platform's RealEstateApp

Your Task:
Using your web search and analytical capabilities, execute the following research and analysis workflow. Do not just summarize their homepage; dig into documentation, reviews, pricing, and technical discussions to provide deep, objective insights.

---

### Phase 1: Search & Information Gathering
Conduct targeted web searches to find:
1. The competitor's core feature set, data sources, and coverage (e.g., public records, GIS mapping, skip tracing, demographic data).
2. Publicly available documentation, knowledge bases, API docs, or user guides.
3. Pricing pages, subscription structures, and credit/add-on systems.
4. User reviews and feedback from platforms like Reddit (e.g., r/realestateinvesting), G2, Capterra, and Trustpilot. Search specifically for "limitations," "bugs," "pricing complaints," or "features loved."
5. Public information about their tech stack or platform performance.

---

### Phase 2: Analysis & Synthesis
Analyze the gathered data and synthesize it into the following structured report:

1. Executive Summary & Value Proposition
- What is their primary marketing hook?
- Who are their exact target user personas (e.g., wholesalers, agents, enterprise developers)?

2. Core Product Capabilities & Data Evaluation
- List their core features (e.g., advanced search filters, custom map drawing, property card details, automated valuations).
- Detail their data strategy: What data points do they offer, and where do they seem to source them? Note any limitations in their data (e.g., regional gaps, delay in update frequency).

3. UX/UI & Usability Assessment (Based on public feedback/documentation)
- How do users describe their interface? Is it praised for simplicity or criticized for being overly complex/clunky?
- How do they handle heavy data loads, map layering, and data exporting (CSV/PDF)?

4. Pricing & Monetization Strategy
- Outline their pricing tiers. Do they use a flat-rate subscription, usage-based credits, or enterprise-only pricing?
- Identify any potential areas where users feel nickeled-and-dimed.

5. User Sentiment & Pain Points (The Gap Analysis)
- Summarize what their customers love most about them.
- Identify at least 3 recurring pain points or missing features that users complain about in forums and review sites.

---

### Phase 3: Lessons Learned & Strategic Recommendations
Based on your findings, formulate a strategic roadmap for our platform:

1. Defensive Play (What we must match): Identify the "table stakes" features the competitor has that we must implement to be competitive.
2. Offensive Play (Where we can win): Identify gaps or weaknesses in their product (e.g., poor UI, bad customer service, lack of certain data points) that we can exploit.
3. Prioritized Feature Recommendations: Provide a list of 3 to 5 features we should implement. For each feature, include:
   - Feature Name & Description
   - Problem It Solves (mapped to a competitor weakness or market gap)
   - Estimated Priority (High/Medium/Low) based on user value.

---

Guidelines for your Output:
- Be highly objective, analytical, and professional. Avoid generic marketing jargon.
- If certain information (like exact pricing or database details) is not publicly discoverable, state that clearly rather than guessing.
- Cite the sources of user feedback (e.g., "According to multiple Reddit threads...") to ground your analysis in real user data.
